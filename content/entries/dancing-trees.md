### Towards Music-Driven Procedural Animation

{{< youtube id=lSEopGiX7L4 >}}

The main video result of my master's thesis, where I investigated audio-visual complementarity and developed an integratetd 3D environment that allowed me to use musical and audio information to direct a procedural animation of a growing tree in the wind.

The thesis was condensed into a paper that can be found [here](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/338600981_Towards_Music-Driven_Procedural_Animation).
